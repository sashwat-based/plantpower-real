🌱 PlantPower AI Chat

Ask about the possible disease, symptoms to observe, watering, soil, and general non-chemical care. This offline chat uses guided responses rather than a generative AI model because no separate AI chat service is connected. PlantPower cannot choose pesticides or provide chemical-use instructions; ask a qualified local agricultural professional about any treatment product.

Do not rely on an AI chat alone for crop-treatment decisions. Confirm important findings with a qualified agricultural expert.

About PlantPower 🌱

Technology for healthier plants and better crop awareness.

Our mission

PlantPower is a free agricultural information platform designed to help people explore crop care, learn about common diseases and organise their farm information.

Free forever design goal

PlantPower is designed without paid subscriptions, paid APIs or compulsory accounts. This prototype stores personal information locally in your browser.

Important information

PlantPower can analyse plant photographs using the Plant.id API. Results are AI-generated suggestions, not confirmed diagnoses. Check important crop-health decisions with a qualified agricultural professional.
